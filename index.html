    Product bets that paid off

    Four decisions: what the situation needed, what I did and what changed.

    Bringing AI inference in-house

    CONTUS TECH, 2025–2026

    AI features had to reach 500+ customers without depending on third-party AI providers. I built the inference strategy around open-source models and self-managed infrastructure, then pitched the AI roadmap to ecosystem partners to fund experimentation.

    • ~40% lower inference cost
    • $20K+ in grants and credits
    • AI at scale across 500+ customers

    One integration layer for every provider

    CONTUS TECH

    Each customer wanted different third-party services. I architected a provider-agnostic integration framework, run from a centralised control plane, so services can be configured and deployed per customer instead of being rebuilt each time.

    • Less integration effort
    • Faster implementation
    • Lower infrastructure cost

    Halving a 12-month roadmap

    CONTUS TECH

    I introduced a hybrid operating model that brings AI-assisted discovery and requirements engineering into Agile delivery, alongside continuous discovery through customer interviews, pilots and usage signals.

    • Production-ready in 6 months, not 12

    Turning product quality around

    PeopleOne Technologies, 2023–2024

    Customer-reported issues were high across the accounts I managed. I ran support-ticket and root-cause analysis, re-prioritised the backlog around the biggest causes and drove fixes across engineering, design and support.

    • Issues down from 60% to 5% in 3 months
    • 40% faster project delivery
    • 25% revenue growth
